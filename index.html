    Five steps, and at every one you can see what is happening.

    You send plans, dimensions, references and wishes.

    I build the 3D scene and we agree on camera angles.

    I show a frame without decor or final lighting.

    Render

    I set up lighting, materials, textiles and decor.

    I make revisions and deliver high-quality files.

    Questions and terms

    Prepayment and contract

    I start after a 50% prepayment, and you pay the rest before receiving the final files. We can sign a contract if you wish.

    What do you need from me to start?

    A plan with dimensions, references, and a list of furniture and materials. If something is missing, I will help.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 3–5 days for a room and 10–14 days for an apartment.

    Usage rights

    You receive the renders for any use. I ask permission to show the work in my portfolio; if you need an NDA, I will follow it.

    What file format do I get?

    High-resolution JPG or PNG, and 4K if needed.

    Do you work with other cities and countries?

    Yes, fully online.
